Tetesum

Tetesum is an 18+ browser puzzle game. Players solve number-sum area puzzles by choosing which numbers to keep and which numbers to erase. Completing stages unlocks AI-generated adult-themed artwork in the gallery.

Game Content

  • The game is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older.
  • All artwork is generated with AI.
  • All depicted characters are intended to be 18 years of age or older.
  • The game runs in the browser and does not require account registration.

How It Works

Each puzzle has row totals, column totals, and color-area totals. Players select numbers so the sums match the required values. Progress is saved locally in the player's browser.
