Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tetesum handles data.

Local Storage

Tetesum stores game progress, settings, and age confirmation status in the user's browser localStorage. This data stays on the user's device and is not saved to a Tetesum account server.

Advertising

The site may use third-party advertising services. These providers may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads.

No Account Registration

The game does not require user account registration and does not ask users to submit personal profiles inside the game.

Clearing Data

Users can clear saved progress by deleting site data in their browser settings.
